Chopping List

One shopping list for the two of you. Tick things off as you shop, keep your staples one tap away, and see when you last bought anything.

Shown next to things you add or tick, so you both know who got what.
or set it up yourself
  1. Create an Airtable base, or reuse the one Do It uses. The app adds its own table called Chopping List.
  2. Create a personal access token at airtable.com/create/tokens with data.records:read, data.records:write, schema.bases:read and schema.bases:write, and give it access to that base.
  3. Paste both below. Then send your partner an invite link from Settings.
